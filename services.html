CLINICAL DIRECTORY

Treatment Procedures & Modalities

Non-Invasive

Plantar Fasciitis ESWT Shockwave

High-energy acoustic pulses stimulate neovascularization and dissolve calcified spurs. 88% pain resolution within 3 to 5 weekly sessions.

Duration: 20 Mins / Session
Downtime: Zero (Walk immediately)
Insurance: Covered by Most PPO
Book This Treatment
Custom Biomechanics

3D Dynamic Laser Orthotics

4,096-sensor dynamic gait scan maps high-pressure hotspots and pronation angles. 3D-printed carbon shell with personalized arch height.

Scan Time: 15 Mins In-Office
Fabrication: 5-7 Business Days
Warranty: 10-Year Shell Warranty
Book Gait Scan
Micro-Invasive

Percutaneous Bunion Surgery

Realignment through micro-punctures under fluoroscopy. No large 7cm cuts. Significantly less post-operative swelling and immediate weight-bearing in a boot.

Anesthesia: Local + Twilight (Outpatient)
Recovery: Walk Day 1 in boot
Scarring: Near-Invisible 3mm mark
Surgical Consult
Sports Lab

Achilles Tendonitis Protocol

Combined focused shockwave, platelet-rich matrix therapy, and eccentric kinetic training to reverse micro-tears and chronic morning stiffness.

Sessions: 4-6 Sessions
Return to Run: Gradual (Week 3-4)
Modality: Non-Surgical
Book Achilles Rehab
Laser Suite

Nd:YAG Fungal Nail Laser

Therapeutic 1064nm wavelength penetrates the keratin plate to destroy fungal spores without damaging the nail matrix or stressing liver enzymes.

Time: 15 Mins (All 10 toes)
Pain Level: Mild warmth only
Success: 92% Clear Nail Regrowth
Book Laser Session
Permanent Relief

Ingrown Toenail Matrixectomy

Painless in-office chemical matrixectomy using phenol. Gently removes the irritating nail border permanently with a 98% non-recurrence rate.

Procedure: 15 Mins In-Office
Relief: Immediate next morning
Recurrence: < 2% Permanent Fix
Book Matrixectomy
