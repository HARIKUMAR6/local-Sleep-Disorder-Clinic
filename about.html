Our Clinical Heritage

Where Biomechanical Science Meets Patient Comfort

Founded in 2008 by Dr. Julian Vance, DPM, FACFAS, StepWell Podiatry was conceived with a clear vision: to bring academic medical center technology directly to an outpatient setting where patients are treated like family.

Over the past 18 years, our practice has grown from a single treatment suite into a state-of-the-art foot & ankle surgical and biomechanical rehabilitation center with 6 private suites, zero-radiation digital fluoroscopy, and an in-house 3D orthotic laboratory.
