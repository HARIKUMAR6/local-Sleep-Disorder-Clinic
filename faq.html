BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

A Simple Way to Prepare for Better Sleep Care.

Knowing what to notice, what to ask, and what to bring can make your first conversation more useful and more comfortable.

3-Stage Pathway
Notice • Ask • Prepare
01 NOTICE

Pay attention to your sleep patterns.

Take note of subtle patterns in your nightly routine: changes in overall sleep quality, difficulty falling or staying asleep, unusual nighttime disturbances, or persistent daytime tiredness. Observing how often these moments occur provides valuable context for your clinical evaluation.

02 ASK

Bring the questions that matter to you.

Reflect on what you have been experiencing, approximately when the concern began, what you would like to understand better, and what you hope to explore during your consultation. Writing down your core questions ensures your personal priorities lead the conversation.

03 PREPARE

Bring useful information to the conversation.

Gather any general observations that help describe your sleep situation: current concerns, questions regarding evaluation options, or existing notes you may already have. Bringing these reflections ensures an unhurried, comfortable start to your care.

PATIENT PREPARATION

Before You Begin.

Preparing for your sleep evaluation helps our clinical team understand your situation clearly. Here are four thoughtful steps to consider before your consultation.

01
Step 01 • Reflection

Think About Your Sleep

Reflect on your typical sleep patterns, morning feelings, daytime alertness, and when concerns first began to be noticeable.

02
Step 02 • Inquiries

Note Your Questions

Write down any specific concerns, nocturnal awakenings, or questions you wish to explore with our clinical specialists.

03
Step 03 • Documentation

Bring Relevant Information

Bring any information that may help you explain your sleep concerns, such as current medication lists or notes from previous evaluations.

04
Step 04 • Collaboration

Start the Conversation

Engage in an unhurried, collaborative discussion with our clinicians to determine what care pathway makes the most sense.
