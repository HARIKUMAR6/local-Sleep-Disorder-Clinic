Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding appointment preparation, insurance coverage, and modern podiatric procedures.

How do custom 3D orthotics differ from store insoles?
Over-the-counter insoles provide generic foam cushioning but cannot correct biomechanical joint angles. Our custom medical orthotics are fabricated using dynamic laser gait mapping to balance your subtalar joint, lift arches precisely, and relieve pressure on tendons and lower back joints.
Is shockwave therapy for plantar fasciitis painful?
Extracorporeal Shockwave Therapy (ESWT) delivers acoustic sound pulses into the inflamed fascia to trigger micro-circulation and stem-cell repair. Patients experience a mild tapping sensation that is well-tolerated. No needles, anesthesia, or downtime are required, and you can immediately resume normal daily activities.
Do you accept Medicare and major PPO insurance plans?
Yes. StepWell Podiatry is an in-network provider with Medicare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare, and most major commercial PPO plans. Our billing team verifies your exact benefits prior to treatment and handles all claims.
What is the recovery time for minimally invasive bunion surgery?
Unlike traditional open surgeries that require large 5-7cm incisions and weeks of cast immobilization, our percutaneous technique uses tiny 3mm entry points. Patients walk out immediately in a post-op boot, experience significantly less swelling, and typically return to supportive sneakers within 3 to 4 weeks.
How often should diabetic patients have their feet examined?
The American Podiatric Medical Association (APMA) strongly recommends a comprehensive podiatric examination at least once per year for all diabetic patients, or every 3 to 6 months if you experience sensory neuropathy, peripheral artery disease (PAD), or previous foot ulcers.
